# Nozzle Design

Define the parameters from openMotor for the fuel.

Parameters to know: $F, P_0, P_e, O/F, T_0, k \text{ and  } R$

Here I will choose KNSB:

- Specific Heat Ratio, $\gamma = 1.1361$
- Combustion Temperature, $T_o = 1520K$
- Exhaust Molar Mass, $M = 39.9 g\cdot mol$

$$
\implies R = \frac{8.31446}{0.0399}=208.3824561404 J/kg\cdot K
$$

- Characteristic Velocity, $c^* = 885m/s$

Take Properties, $()_0 \approx ()_1$

## Conical

Throat temperature, $T^* = \frac{2 \cdot T_1}{k+1}$

Throat Pressure, 

$$
P^* = P_1 \left(1+ \frac{\gamma -1 }{2}\right)^{-\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}
$$

Ideal thrust Coefficient, 

$$
C_{f,ideal} = \sqrt{\frac{2\gamma^2}{\gamma - 1} \left( \frac{2}{\gamma + 1} \right)^{\frac{\gamma + 1}{\gamma - 1}} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac{P_e}{P_c}\right)^{\frac{\gamma - 1}{\gamma}} \right]}
$$

Actual thrust coefficient,

$$
\lambda = \frac{1 + \cos(\alpha^\circ)}{2}
\\C_{f,\text{actual}} = \lambda \cdot C_f
$$

Throat area, derived using m*=rho*A*v*=rho*A*a

$$
A_t = \frac{\dot{m} \sqrt{R T_c}}{P_c \sqrt{\gamma \left( \frac{2}{\gamma + 1} \right)^{\frac{\gamma + 1}{\gamma - 1}}}}
$$

From area find D_t

P_e /P_optimum,

$$
P_{optimum} = \frac23 (P_{low} - P_{high})
$$

Exit mach no,

$$
M_e = \sqrt{\frac{2}{\gamma - 1} \left[ \left(\frac{P_c}{P_e}\right)^{\frac{\gamma - 1}{\gamma}} - 1 \right]}
$$

Expansion ratio,

$$
\epsilon = \frac{A_e}{A_t} = \frac{1}{M_e} \left[ \frac{2}{\gamma + 1} \left( 1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2} M_e^2 \right) \right]^{\frac{\gamma + 1}{2(\gamma - 1)}} 
$$

From A_e find exit diameter

Find diverging length,

$$
L_d = \frac{r_e - r_t}{ \tan(\alpha^\circ)}
$$

Throat Velocity, $a=\sqrt{kRT}$

Exit Velocity, 

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2k}{k-1} R T_0 \left[ 1 - \left(\frac{p_2}{p_0}\right)^{\frac{k-1}{k}} \right]}
$$

For the contour nozzle see Huzel & Huang page 76.
